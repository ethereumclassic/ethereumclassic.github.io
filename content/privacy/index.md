Last updated: November 16, 2026

This website (ethereumclassic.org) is a static, informational community site. It does not require accounts and does not collect personal information directly from visitors.

## Data We Process

- **Server logs** (via Netlify): When you visit the site, Netlify’s content delivery network processes standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, pages requested, and timestamps. This is used for security, performance, abuse prevention, and optional site analytics.
- **Search**: On-site search is powered by Algolia. Queries are processed to return results; we do not store personal profiles.
- **No cookies for tracking**: The site itself sets no cookies for analytics or advertising. Netlify’s analytics are server-side and do not use cookies.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not run advertising or cross-site tracking.

## Legal Basis and Purpose

Processing is based on legitimate interest in operating, securing, and understanding usage of a public informational website. Netlify states that its server-side analytics (no cookies, no personally identifying information in the analytics data) is GDPR-compliant.

## Data Sharing and Processors

- **Netlify** hosts the site and processes the server logs described above. See [Netlify’s Privacy Policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/) and their [GDPR information](https://www.netlify.com/gdpr-ccpa/).
- **Algolia** powers site search. See [Algolia’s Privacy Policy](https://www.algolia.com/policies/privacy/).

No other third parties receive personal data from this site.

## Retention

Netlify retains logs according to its own policies (typically short periods for operational needs). We do not maintain separate long-term personal data stores.

## Your Rights

If you are in the EEA, UK, or other jurisdictions with similar rules, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, or to object to processing based on legitimate interest. Because this site stores almost no personal data itself, most requests will be limited. Contact us and we will forward relevant requests to Netlify where applicable.

## Contact

This is a community-maintained site with no single legal entity. For privacy questions or requests, open an issue on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/ethereumclassic/ethereumclassic.github.io) or reach out in the [ETC Discord](https://ethereumclassic.org/community/channels).

## Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top will change when we do.
